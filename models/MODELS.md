# 3D model files

| File | What | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| `build_panel.py` | Parametric Blender build of the whole panel from the BSD-PFS-003 numbers. Pass 1–3: case, hinged lid, plate with holes, gauges, valves, couplings, digital gauge, engraved labels, bands, fill whip, lights, cameras. Pass 4: ¼ in tube runs under the plate to the P&ID (`build_piping`), painted flow mimic with arrows and 5 mm tags (`build_mimic`), three cascade sets with 47 L cylinders, pigtails, check valves, tee chains, bleeds and whips (`build_cascade`), brushed/textured materials + HDRI (`realism`). Pass 5: enamel cylinder paints, whips draped to the floor and over the front rim (`whip_path`), top camera offset so its roll is defined, cascade view rendered 0.5 stop darker. Pass 6: a 1000 × 700 × 760 stainless work bench (`build_bench`, node `Bench`); the case assembly, focus, panel cameras and lights are lifted by `TABLE_H`, the cascade cylinders and the 3 L fill cylinder stay on the floor, and both whip sets are rerouted (`whip_path` and `reroute_fill_whip` branch on `TABLE_H`; set it to 0 for the floor layout). Pass 7: mimic redrawn to the P&ID (gauge taps upstream of the valves, gaps at non-junction crossings), labels placed clear of lines and neighbours, `mimic_report()` added. Run phase by phase: `exec(open(path).read()); M = materials(); run('case')` … `run('piping')`, `run('mimic')`, `run('cascade')`, `run('realism')`, `run('under')`, `run('cascade_render')`, `run('bench')`, `run('report')` (orthographic plate render + GA-mm table of labels and mimic segments — check it before publishing), `run('save')`, then `run('convert')` and `run('export')` last (conversion destroys the editable text, so never save after it; the conversion parents first and reports any object whose world matrix moved — read that line). After every export open the live viewer, not just the renders: the renders are made before conversion and cannot show export faults. | Blender 5.1 via the MCP bridge, 2026-09-04 |

Lessons recorded in the build (see also ../../MISTAKES.md):
- A solid "rim lip" slab across the case opening hid every part shorter than 36 mm — build rims as rings.
- A TRACK_TO camera placed dead above its target has an undefined roll; the top view came out rotated ~60° until the camera was offset 120 mm towards the front.
- Cylinder paints at metallic 0.3 under the studio HDRI all read as white; enamel is metallic ≈0. When colours look wrong, render once with flat diagnostic colours (red/green/blue per material) before touching lighting — it separates "wrong material" from "exposure" in one 5 s render.
