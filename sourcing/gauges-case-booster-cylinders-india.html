5. Breathing-air source in India

232 bar is the norm. Indian dive centres run 200–232 bar steel/aluminium cylinders, and no published Indian dive centre was found advertising a 300 bar fill service. 300/330 bar exists in India only as equipment: Bauer (Mariner 200, to 330 bar) and Coltri machines and 330 bar refill adapters are sold through Indian distributors (Bhavnagar, Chennai, Auroville, Ernakulam), so a 300 bar supply is something you would have to own, not something you can assume finding. Sources: Bauer, IndiaMART breathing-air compressors. Confidence: checked for equipment availability; negative result on fill-service availability — absence of evidence, not proof of absence.

Design-basis implications

  1. Three different bullnoses, not one. O₂ = G 5/8 RH, He/inert = G 3/4 RH, air = G 7/8 RH, all right-hand. The panel needs three distinct supply whips/adapters. Any assumption of a single 5/8 BSP supply fitting is wrong for helium and for air.
  2. O₂ arrives at ~147 bar, not 150–200 bar. The Indian 46.7 L jumbo is filled to 150 kgf/cm² = 147 bar at 15 °C. Revise the O₂ supply assumption downward. PP blending above ~140 bar O₂ partial requires the booster, not cascade.
  3. He is 150–200 bar depending on what you order. The 200 bar assumption holds only if it is specified per order; 150 bar 47 L fills are also sold. Confirm with each supplier.
  4. Air: hold the design basis at 232 bar. Treat 300 bar as an optional capability requiring owned equipment, not a field-available supply.
  5. The 0–400 bar NS 63 gauge is only rated for 300 bar steady. WIKA NS 63 pressure limits are ¾ FS steady (300 bar), ⅔ FS fluctuating (267 bar), FS short-time. At a 232 bar basis this is fine; if the air line ever runs at 300 bar the gauge is at its steady limit and above its fluctuating limit. Either cap air at 232 bar, or move the air gauge to 0–600 bar and accept the resolution loss.
  6. CPG500 is out — no 400 bar range, 0.25 % FS (±0.875 bar on the nearest range), NBR seals. Digital choice is CPG1500 with the factory "oxygen, oil and grease free" option, or a Keller LEO5 0–400 bar that would need third-party O₂ cleaning (Keller offers no O₂ option).
  7. XBD30-OL likely stalls near 240 bar (30:1 ratio × 8 bar max drive, per USUN's own rule). That is marginal for topping a 232 bar cylinder and insufficient for 300 bar. Either move up to GB40-OL / AGB30-OL-F (both catalogued at 300 bar out), or buy domestically: Maximator India, Navi Mumbai, already lists a "Rebreather Oxygen Booster" and its DLE series is rated for oxygen to 350 bar — that removes the import, the duty question, and the service problem.
  8. Case: ₹42,000 for the Pelican 1550 vs ₹6,680–7,050 for an Indian MAX/Unicase equivalent at the same interior. That is a ~6× premium for the brand; worth an explicit decision.
