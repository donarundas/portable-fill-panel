Portable Fill Station — Module A fill panel P&ID

A partial-pressure decant and blending panel in a Pelican 1550. Three labelled quick couplings bring in an O₂ cascade, a He cascade and an air cylinder; a common manifold feeds either the fill line directly or an external USUN XBD30-OL booster in its own case. Fill zone 200 bar, relief at 220 bar, bleed valve before the product coupling. Whole panel oxygen-service.

How a fill works

1Connect and check

All needle valves closed. Supplies mate on their labelled couplings QC-01 / 02 / 03 pressure-free — match the colour band on the whip to the band on the port. The couplings are not keyed, so the label is the check. Read PI-01..03. Connect the fill cylinder to QC-06 through the DIN whip and open its valve: PI-04 now reads the cylinder.

2Direct decant

NV-04 DIRECT open, NV-05 shut. Meter O₂ on NV-01 while watching PI-04 to the target partial pressure; shut. Then He on NV-02, then air top-up on NV-03. CV-01..03 stop the higher-pressure supply backing into the others. Cascade rule: one supply cylinder valve open at a time, lowest pressure first.

3Booster mode

When the supply is at or below cylinder pressure: shut NV-04, open NV-05, run BST-01 on drive gas from a scuba first stage set near 7 bar. Gas leaves through QC-04, returns boosted through QC-05 and CV-04 into the fill line. Stop at target. At 7 bar drive the 30:1 booster stalls below the 220 bar relief; PSV-01 is the last resort, not the stop.

4Finish and disconnect

Shut the cylinder valve and bleed the whip at its DIN adaptor. Open NV-04 and the fill-line bleed V-01 to empty the panel through VB-01, then shut everything. Then bleed each inlet leg by opening its needle valve with NV-04 and V-01 open, and part the couplings. Nothing is mated or parted under pressure; the bleed does the venting, not the coupling.

5Analyse and label

Analyse O₂ and He at the cylinder valve after the mix settles, then label the cylinder. There is no inline analyser on this revision; the analyser travels in its own case.

Decisions locked 2026-09-04

  1. Fill zone 200 bar, PSV-01 set 220 bar.
  2. One keyed inlet per gas; the cascade is an external pigtail manifold on the cylinders.
  3. One fill-line bleed V-01 just before QC-06 depressurises the whole panel, inlet legs included, through the open needle valves and check valves. The fill whip bleeds at its DIN adaptor; each cascade chain has a bleed after its last tee. The couplings therefore need no vent of their own (revised at review).
  4. Booster USUN XBD30-OL, in its own Pelican case.
  5. Pelican 1550 holds the panel only. Booster and analyser each travel in their own case.
  6. Master gauge digital.
  7. Inline analyser module and diver gas panel removed from scope. The analysis port shown on SK-A is deleted.
  8. Needle valves rated 344 bar (Swagelok SS-1RS4 class) accepted against the 300 bar maximum supply; every other component stays ≥414 bar.
  9. Standard Swagelok or FAV parts, oxygen-cleaned in-house with FKM/PTFE seals and Krytox, instead of factory SC-11 oxygen variants.
  10. Quick couplings: FAV FDQRCO2 double-check couplings, not keyed, on ¼ in NPT bulkhead adaptors; ports labelled and colour-banded. The WEH quote is kept only as a comparison.

Confirm before purchase

  • FAV FDQRCO2: gas service and leak-tightness at 300 bar with helium, seal materials (FKM or PTFE), poppet spring material, locking mechanism, cycle life, and the rule for mating and parting (we bleed to zero first). Fallback if it fails: DIN 477 handwheels.
  • In-house oxygen-cleaning procedure: standard Swagelok and FAV parts arrive with hydrocarbon lubricant. Each valve is disassembled, degreased, fitted with FKM or PTFE seals (aftermarket Viton where an O-ring exists), re-lubricated with Krytox or Christo-Lube and UV-inspected before assembly. Write the procedure down before the first oxygen fill. Also whether Monel or brass trim is preferable at the throttling points.
  • PSV-01 spring range at 220 bar and relief capacity against the XBD30-OL's flow.
  • XBD30-OL stall-pressure curve, to fix the R-01 set point that keeps stall below 220 bar.
  • Pelican 1550 depth against the gauge and needle-valve stack (cardboard mock-up), see the 3D arrangement.
  • Whip hose O₂ rating at 300 bar (Hydroflex Pipe, Ahmedabad, lists 400 bar DN6 assemblies but not oxygen). Cylinder outlets on the supplier's actual cylinders: IS 3224 No. 3 (O₂), No. 20 (He), No. 19 (air) assumed.
