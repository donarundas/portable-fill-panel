Depth check

  • Body interior depth 149 mm. Plate top at 119 mm above the floor (30 mm below the rim), so 115 mm clear under the plate for valve bodies, check valves, the relief valve and ¼ in tube runs. A Swagelok N-series body with two tube fittings is about 70 mm deep.
  • Above the plate the tallest items are the digital gauge (about 80 mm) and the needle-valve knobs (about 55 mm). Both sit inside the 30 mm rim plus the 44 mm lid recess, so the lid closes with the whips coiled in the lid organiser.
  • The vent bulkhead exits the back wall behind the plate, below the rim, so the discharge is outside the case with the lid open or shut.

Generated part meshes

The gauges, needle valves, couplings and digital gauge are loaded from models/<slot>.glb when those files exist. They were produced on the home GPU box by Hunyuan3D (via Modly) and simplified for the web; the viewer rescales each mesh by its bounding box to the slot size, so absolute scale in the file does not matter but +Y must be up. The first set (2026-09-04) was generated from AI-drawn stand-in images because the vendor sites blocked photo downloads, so the shapes are generic and untextured and lose the gas colour coding. The page therefore shows the procedural parts by default; the toggle swaps the generated meshes in for comparison. Regenerating from real product photographs (WIKA 232.50, Swagelok SS-1RS4 or FAV FNV, FAV FDQRCO2, WIKA CPG1500) is the step that would make them representative. Provenance per slot is in models/MODELS.md.

What this pass does not decide

  • Exact coupling and gauge models — the geometry uses class-level envelopes. Positions are set with 75 mm pitch so any of the candidate couplings fits.
  • Tube routing under the plate. It follows the P&ID and is drawn at the fabrication stage once the valve models are fixed.
  • Case colour and lid organiser. Shown black; a yellow or orange case is the usual choice for a boat deck.
