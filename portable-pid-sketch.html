Portable Fill Station — Module A fill panel

A partial-pressure decant and blending panel in a Pelican case: three keyed HP inlets (O₂ cascade, He cascade, air), a common manifold, one product outlet, and a booster loop switched in by two needle valves. Check the topology here; the final P&ID (REV A) follows your answers to the decisions below.

How a fill works

1Connect and check

All needle valves closed. Supplies go on their keyed couplings QC-O2 / QC-HE / QC-AIR — a He whip cannot land on the O₂ port. Read the three supply gauges. Connect the fill cylinder to QC-OUT and open its valve: PI-04 now reads the cylinder.

2Direct decant

NV-04 DIRECT open, NV-05 shut. Meter O₂ on NV-01 while watching PI-04 up to the target partial pressure; shut. Then He on NV-02, then air top-up on NV-03. Each inlet's check valve stops the higher-pressure gas backing into the other bottles. Cascade rule: draw from the lowest supply cylinder first.

3Booster mode

When the supply is at or below cylinder pressure: shut NV-04, open NV-05, start the booster on drive gas from a scuba first stage. Gas leaves through QC-BS, returns boosted through QC-BD and CV-04 into the output line. Stop at target; the booster sweeps supplies down to about 25 bar. PSV-01 is the last resort, not the stop.

4Analyse and label

Today: hand-held O₂/He analyser at the cylinder valve after settling, then a contents label. Later: Module B plugs onto the plugged port AN and reads the product inline through a regulator and restrictor, as FO-01 does on the fixed plant.

5Shut down

Close the cylinder valve, vent the output whip on V-04, bleed each inlet leg on V-01..03, then part the couplings pressure-free. Every vent leaves the case through one bulkhead port. The lid stays open whenever gas is flowing, so an O₂ leak cannot enrich the case.

Decisions needed before REV A

  1. Fill pressure class. 232-bar cylinders with PSV-01 at 250 bar (recommended: matches a 3 L / AL40 / twinset fleet and the fixed plant's DIN 232 whips), or 300-bar class with PSV-01 at 330 bar.
  2. Inlets. One keyed inlet per gas with the cascade on an external pigtail manifold (drawn), or two inlets per gas on the panel.
  3. Inlet bleed. Per-leg bleed valves V-01..03 (drawn), or couplings with an integrated vent once the QC family is confirmed.
  4. Booster. USUN GBD40-OL or Haskel AGT-15/30-O₂ — sets maximum fill pressure, weight and price.
  5. Case. Pelican 1550 for the panel alone, or 1600 so Module B can share the case.
  6. Master gauge. Digital (drawn) or 100 mm analogue class 1.0.

Confirm before REV A

  • Panel-side HP quick-coupling family: pressure rating, O₂-cleaning, keyed variants, mating under residual pressure. If nothing qualifies, the inlets revert to DIN 477 handwheels and only the cylinder ends get quick connectors.
  • Needle valve model and SC-11 cleaning; whether Monel or brass trim is preferable at the throttling points (EIGA IGC 13 velocity guidance vs. the dive-industry 316 SS norm).
  • PSV model and spring range at the chosen set pressure.
  • Pelican 1550 interior depth against the gauge and needle-valve stack (cardboard mock-up).
  • Whip hose O₂ rating at 300 bar; Indian O₂ cylinder valve outlet for the cascade pigtails.

References

  • WEH gas quick connectors overview — TW17 350 bar (oxygen applications), TW117 450 bar.
  • Stäubli RBL stainless couplings — 300–450 bar by size; O₂ cleaning to be confirmed.
  • Swagelok N-series needle valves · CH check valves · R3A/R4 relief valves — all 6000 psig (413 bar).
  • Haskel AG series — AG-15-O₂ tops out at 155 bar, so it is not a candidate. AGT-15/30-O₂ — 345 bar, 18 kg.
  • USUN GBD40-OL — 332 bar, 8 bar drive, 25 bar minimum inlet. DGX on drive gas — driven from a scuba first-stage LP port.
  • Kirby Morgan KMACS-5 manual — the portable air-panel reference: 21 × 18 × 11.5 in, 54 lb, 8–15.5 bar umbilical. Amron 8225-HP — 19 × 14 × 11.5 in two-diver panel.
