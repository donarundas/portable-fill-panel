Assembly and test sequence

  1. Drill pilot holes only until the purchased parts are in hand, then open A–F to the vendor drawings. Anodise and engrave after drilling.
  2. Oxygen-clean assembly of standard parts: disassemble each valve, degrease, fit FKM or PTFE seals, re-lubricate with Krytox or Christo-Lube only, and inspect under UV and white light to ASTM G93 / CGA G-4.1 before it goes on. Clean bench, lint-free gloves, no hydrocarbons.
  3. Fit gauges, valves and couplings to the plate; route ¼ in tube under the plate per BSD-PFS-001, at least 15 mm above the base plate, clipped every 200 mm.
  4. Leak test with dry nitrogen: inlet legs and manifold at 330 bar (1.1 × maximum supply), fill line at 250 bar with PSV-01 blanked. Soap or an oxygen-compatible detector on every joint. Restore PSV-01 and confirm lift at about 220 bar.
  5. Purge with nitrogen, cap every coupling, fit the assembly into the case on its base plate, connect VB-01 through the back wall.

Confirm before cutting

  • Hole diameters B, C, D and F against the drawings of the parts actually bought; only A and E are catalogue values.
  • Gauge U-clamp envelope at 75 mm pitch, or widen the gauge row to 80 mm pitch and let the colour bands jog.
  • Digital gauge height (≈80 mm) against the 30 mm rim gap plus 44 mm lid recess: it clears by about 6 mm on the LEO5; check the CPG1500.
  • Pelican 1550 actual interior at the plate height — the walls taper; measure before cutting the plate.
